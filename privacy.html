BunkerBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BunkerBot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when operating on Discord. BunkerBot provides chat-related commands, message logging and tracking, and automated spam detection and moderation.

1. Information Collected

BunkerBot logs messages in Discord channels it can access. Information collected and stored includes:

  • Display names: Discord display names associated with messages and interactions.
  • Message content: Messages sent in channels the Bot can access, where its message logging functionality is active.

The Bot may process limited technical information required to receive messages, execute commands, associate records with the appropriate users, and perform moderation actions. The precise information processed may depend on Discord's event data and the Bot's implementation.

2. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to:

  • Maintain message logs and support message tracking.
  • Process commands and provide the Bot's features.
  • Detect suspected spam accounts and automated spam activity.
  • Support server moderation, including banning accounts identified as spam bots.
  • Troubleshoot issues and maintain the Bot's operation.

Message content is used for the Bot's stated functionality and other purposes permitted by applicable law and Discord's policies.

3. Message Logging and Data Retention

Messages in Discord channels the Bot can access are logged. Stored message logs do not have a routine automatic deletion schedule and are retained until manually deleted by the Bot operator or deleted in response to an applicable deletion requirement.

This retention practice does not override applicable legal obligations or Discord's Developer Terms, including requirements to delete information when required or when it is no longer necessary for the Bot's permitted functionality.

Users may request deletion of information associated with them by contacting bunker_14 through Discord, as described in Section 8. A message deleted from Discord may still exist in a separately stored log unless that log is also deleted.

4. Data Sharing

BunkerBot's operator does not sell message content or display names or share them for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by hosting, database, or other service providers where necessary to operate the Bot, or disclosed where required or permitted by law.

5. Data Security

The operator should use reasonable safeguards to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. User Requests and Deletion

Users may contact the operator to ask questions about information held by the Bot or request access to, correction of, or deletion of their information. Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and Discord's policies. Where deletion is required, the relevant information will be deleted promptly, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

To make a request, contact bunker_14 through Discord.

7. Discord and Third-Party Services

BunkerBot operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Other services used to host or operate the Bot may have their own privacy terms.
